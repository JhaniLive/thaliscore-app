Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

NutriScan grades food from A to E. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. This page explains exactly what leaves your phone and why.

What stays on your phone

Your scan history, including small thumbnails of photos you scanned, is stored only on your device. Clearing history in the app, or uninstalling it, deletes it.

Barcode scans

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, an open food database, to look up the product. No photo is sent. Open Food Facts’ own privacy policy applies to that request.

Label and food photos

When you scan a nutrition label or a plate of food, the photo is sent to the NutriScan server, which passes it to an AI model to read the label or recognise the dish:

  • The server runs on Cloudflare Workers and does not store your photos.
  • The photo is processed by an AI model through OpenRouter. NutriScan currently uses free open models (such as Alibaba’s Qwen and Google’s Gemma); the providers of free models may keep requests to improve their services. OpenRouter’s and the provider’s privacy policies apply to that processing.
  • Only the photo and the resulting nutrient values are sent. Nothing that identifies you is attached.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes, labels and food.
  • Photos: only when you choose to pick an image from your gallery.

Children

NutriScan doesn’t knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Grades are general guidance based on the Nutri-Score method. Grades for food photos are estimates. For dietary or medical needs, talk to a doctor or dietitian.
